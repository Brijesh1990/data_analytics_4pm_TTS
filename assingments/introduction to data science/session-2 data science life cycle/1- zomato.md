## Problem Definition: Improving Zomato Restaurant Recommendations

Zomato can use data science to recommend restaurants and dishes that are more relevant to each user. The problem is to predict which restaurants a user is most likely to enjoy and order from, based on their preferences, location, budget, and previous orders.

### Business Goal

Increase user satisfaction and the number of successful orders by showing personalized, high-quality recommendations. This can also help restaurants receive more relevant customers and improve customer retention.

### Data Required

- User order history, including restaurants, dishes, order times, ratings, and reviews
- User location and the distance to available restaurants
- Restaurant details, such as cuisine, price range, ratings, delivery time, and availability
- Search, click, view, and add-to-cart history
- Information about discounts, offers, and delivery charges
- Order outcomes, such as completed, cancelled, or returned orders
